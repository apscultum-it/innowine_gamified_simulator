| **Total** | **15 complete cases** | **5 indicators** | **49** | **147 distinct answer feedback items** |

The order is designed for a guided learning journey, but the game allows access to any scenario so facilitators can select themes. First/last error reduction is calculated when both assessments have been completed (not necessarily in strict chronological order). For a controlled trial, instructors should require scenario 01 before teaching and scenario 15 after teaching.

All company scenarios and scoring outcomes are **hypothetical teaching material**, not a claim that the project has collected or validated matching operational case studies. The content follows the agreed WP3 domains. Scientific, VET and industry review is mandatory before finalisation.
